### Hi there,

I'm Joel, VP of Engineering at [Teya](https://www.teya.com/), where I lead the Cloud Platform organisation. For twenty years I've worked on infrastructure, reliability and security: from the certificate authorities behind Portugal's citizen card, to a fully automated private cloud at Paddy Power Betfair, observability at Farfetch, and today a platform shipping ~15,000 deploys a month.

This blog is where I've shared what I learned along the way, mostly hands-on posts on Linux, automation, containers and monitoring. I also wrote [Hands-On Infrastructure Monitoring with Prometheus](/publications/).

When the pagers are silent, I'm running or surfing 🤙, with [Byte Von Snort](https://www.instagram.com/bytevonsnort) strictly enforcing his SLO on walks. More at [kintoandar.com](https://www.kintoandar.com/).
